Vocabulary marking shows words from the Weave Review word bank in EPUB body text and Markdown preview, and lets you add selections to the vocabulary deck. Good for collecting words while reading foreign text and reviewing later in Weave Review. Requires reader premium support; adding to the vocabulary list requires Weave Review installed. The reader no longer ships a separate sidebar vocabulary list—body marking and selection capture are the path. Details below:

## 1. Master Switch
In reader settings **Vocabulary** (or Basic):

1. **Enable vocabulary marking**: Master switch. When off, body marking menu and selection **Add to word bank** entries hide—good if you do not need vocabulary learning at all.
2. On by default. Turn off for a cleaner UI when unused.

## 2. Body Vocabulary Marking
1. Turn on **Body vocabulary marking** or menu **Enable marking**.
2. In EPUB body and Markdown reading / live preview, bank words get underline-style marks.
3. Keeping it off for casual reading is fine; turn on for a close-reading chapter.
4. You can show **New**, **Learning**, **Mature**, and **Due for review** separately.
5. **Mature** may be off by default; if words exist but you see none, try enabling it.

## 3. Show Together with Excerpts
**Show together with excerpt notes** decides whether vocabulary lines and excerpt color blocks appear at once.

1. On: Vocabulary marks and highlights show together.
2. Off: When vocabulary marking is on, excerpt re-highlight in the body is temporarily hidden; the sidebar excerpt list is unaffected.

## 4. Add Words from the Book
1. Select a word or phrase (double-click lookup may also be available, per UI).
2. On the toolbar, choose add to Weave Review vocabulary (label may be **Vocabulary**).
3. Default deck name is often **Vocabulary**; follow the actual picker.
4. Words already in the bank are reported as existing.
5. Without Weave Review installed, you are told the vocabulary list is unavailable.

> Note: Community plugins such as fingertip translation can offer **Add to Weave Review** in lookup popovers—complementary to reader capture. See `We-01a 新建卡片`. Word-list management and review queues live in Weave Review, not a reader sidebar list.

## 5. Word-Card Popover
Clicking a marked word may open a card.

1. You can rate **Remember / Fuzzy / Forgot** (needs Weave Review review).
2. Settings can toggle **Show review interval** above the buttons.
3. The card context menu can also toggle interval display.
