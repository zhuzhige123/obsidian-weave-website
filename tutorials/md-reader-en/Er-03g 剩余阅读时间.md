Remaining reading time shows a sticker estimate for how long is left in the chapter or whole book, adapting as you page and as pace changes. Useful for “can I finish this chapter in twenty minutes?” Mostly premium; may be time-limited. Details below:

## 1. Show the Sticker
1. Open a book in the reader.
2. In the pane menu, find **Remaining reading time**.
3. Toggle the sticker and choose chapter / whole-book scope when offered.
4. When on, a duration sticker appears near the top-right.

## 2. How the Estimate Moves
1. It adjusts with paging, scrolling, and reading pace—not a fixed “pages ÷ minutes” formula.
2. Right after open or a chapter change, the number may start coarse then refine.
3. After a long break, it re-settles to the new pace.

> Note: The sticker is planning aid, not an exam countdown. Close reading, lookups, and note-taking make wall-clock time diverge from the sticker—that is expected.

## 3. Layout and Wiggle
1. The menu may offer sticker layout options (e.g. top sticker); follow the live menu.
2. **Sticker shake / wiggle** can be turned off if distracting—keep the number only.
3. Resume badges and other top hints can coexist with the remaining-time sticker. See `Er-01c 书签、进度与参考阅读点`.

## 4. vs Tomato and Progress Scale
1. **Pomodoro focus timer**: how long you focused. See `Er-03f 番茄专注计时`.
2. **Remaining reading time**: how long is left looking forward.
3. **Bottom progress scale**: whole-book position and scrubbing. See `Er-03i 阅读进度刻度条`.

## 5. Common Cases
1. No sticker: confirm the premium capability and that display is on in the menu.
2. Large jumps in the number: common right after chapter or layout changes; paging further usually stabilizes it.
3. Chapter only: switch scope to the current chapter when the menu offers it.
