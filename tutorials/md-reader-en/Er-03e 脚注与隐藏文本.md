Academic books and textbooks often have footnotes and endnotes. The reader can pop a floating panel when you tap a reference number, or jump to the note location. Strikethrough and hidden text help close reading by temporarily hiding rejected sentences. Footnote popover preview is mostly premium support. Details below:

## 1. Tap Footnote Reference
**Tap footnote reference** in the menu decides what happens when you click a number.

1. **Show footnote popover**: Read the note without leaving the body.
2. **Jump to footnote location**: Scroll to end-of-chapter or where the note lives.
3. Popover title may distinguish **Footnote** vs **Endnote**.


## 2. Popover vs Jump
1. Quick source check or skim the note: use popover.
2. Long note you also want to select from: jump to footnote location.
3. If neither finds content, the book may use plain parentheses instead of standard footnote structure.


## 3. Hide Strikethrough Text
If strikethrough means **this passage is void**, the body looks messy.

1. Turn on **Hide strikethrough text** or related **Hide text** options.
2. When checking the original, enable **Show hidden text and strikethrough in sidebar**.
3. Display may be inline, right column, etc.—per menu.


## 4. Top Badge
Reference reading points can use a top badge, with optional **Sticker shake effect**.

1. Turn off shake if it distracts; keep the position hint.
2. Badges are unrelated to footnotes—they share reading decoration settings.
