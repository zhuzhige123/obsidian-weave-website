The bottom reading progress scale helps you sense position across the **whole book**, scrub to jump, and optionally show chapter nodes and a resume pin. Mostly premium; may be time-limited. Details below:

## 1. Show and Hide
1. Open a book in the reader.
2. In **page-turn** mode, open the bottom page-jump panel, or use the related menu items.
3. Toggle **Show progress scale** / **Hide progress scale**.
4. When hidden, previous/next screen and page jump can remain—only the whole-book scale track is gone.

> Note: The scale is whole-book progress feel; fine chapter structure and paragraph-level resume still live in the TOC and `Er-01c 书签、进度与参考阅读点`.

## 2. Style: Dock Scale and Hairline
Choose one progress style:

1. **Dock scale**: a clearer track and ticks—good when you scrub often.
2. **Hairline**: a quieter thin line—less visual weight.

Switch under **Progress bar style** in the jump panel or menu.

## 3. Chapter Nodes and Resume Pin
1. The scale can show chapter nodes for coarse jumps near a chapter.
2. If a paragraph-level resume is pinned, a resume pin may appear; tap it to return to that spot.
3. The pin and the top resume badge share the same resume anchor—not two competing progress systems. See `Er-01c 书签、进度与参考阅读点`.

## 4. Progress Tone Colors
1. Toggle **Progress tone colors** for color shifts that hint how far you are (exact palette per UI).
2. Off keeps the scale quieter—position and nodes only.
3. Tone colors are visual only; they do not change the real percentage or the resume pin.

## 5. Jumping with the Scale
1. Drag the scrubber (or tap the track) to jump to that whole-book progress.
2. Tap a chapter node for a coarse land near that chapter.
3. Tap the resume pin to return to the pinned paragraph.
4. For an exact chapter title, still click the name in the sidebar TOC.

## 6. vs Other Progress Features
1. **Progress scale**: whole-book browse and scrub (this page).
2. **Paragraph-level resume**: select text to pin an exact resume sentence. See `Er-01c`.
3. **Remaining reading time**: estimate how long is left. See `Er-03g 剩余阅读时间`.
4. **Pomodoro focus timer**: how long you focused this session. See `Er-03f 番茄专注计时`.
5. **Continuous-scroll excerpt marker strip**: in-chapter highlight hints—not a whole-book track. See `Er-01b 阅读器界面与阅读模式`.

## 7. Common Cases
1. Cannot find the toggle: confirm page-turn mode, then open the page-jump panel or pane menu.
2. Premium prompt: follow license / time-limited access messaging.
3. No resume pin: select text, pin as resume, then show the scale.
4. Drag lands mid-chapter off the title: the scale is progress ratio—use TOC or the resume pin for precision.
