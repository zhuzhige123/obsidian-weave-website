Paragraph reading focuses attention on the current paragraph—good for close reading, difficult sentences, and chapters you want to slow down. Immersive fullscreen hides Obsidian chrome and leaves only the body. Translucency gradient adds atmosphere to regular reading. These three are mostly premium support. Details below:

## 1. Paragraph Reading Mode
1. In reader settings, confirm **Enable paragraph reading mode** is on. When off, menu entries disappear.
2. Open a book and turn on **Paragraph mode** from the menu or top bar.
3. In this mode the UI focuses the current paragraph; you can turn screens within the paragraph and move to previous / next paragraph.
4. You can fine-tune font size for this mode (per on-screen controls).
5. Exit to return to page-turn or continuous scroll.


## 2. Paragraph Interface Style
**Paragraph interface style** in the menu switches the look.

1. **Focus halo**: Current paragraph stands out more.
2. **Blend into background**: More restrained, less decoration.
3. **Dashed border**: Outlines the current paragraph with a line frame.


## 3. Paragraph Transition Effects
**Paragraph transition effects** control motion between paragraphs—e.g. steady, fade, light drop, horizontal slide, slide in, lift, drift, settle, expand, curtain, pulse, and more.

1. Pick an effect that does not make you dizzy or feel sluggish.
2. For efficiency, **Steady** works well.
3. **No paragraph to jump to** means chapter structure is not suitable for paragraph mode—exit and use normal reading.


## 4. Random Paragraph
Some interfaces offer **Random paragraph** to re-read one segment.

1. Good for review or breaking sequential reading habit.
2. When no paragraph is available, you are told it cannot be used.


## 5. Immersive Fullscreen
1. Use **Enter immersive fullscreen**.
2. Obsidian UI chrome hides for focus.
3. **Exit immersive fullscreen** anytime to restore normal layout.


## 6. Regular Reading Translucency Gradient
In non-fullscreen regular reading, you can overlay a translucency gradient on the chapter surface.

1. Toggle **Regular reading translucency gradient** in the menu.
2. This is separate from immersive fullscreen—it affects regular reading atmosphere only.
