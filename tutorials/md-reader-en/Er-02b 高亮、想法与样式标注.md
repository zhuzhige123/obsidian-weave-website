After selecting text in a book, you can add color highlights, line-style marks, and a short **thought**. Styles travel with the excerpt into notes and re-appear in the body when possible. Good for separating key points, doubts, and sentences to refine later. Details below:

## 1. Five-Color Highlights
Pick a highlight color when creating an excerpt.

1. Select text and choose a color on the toolbar.
2. Five common colors help you scan categories quickly in notes and the sidebar.
3. On export, color names may appear in some templates (e.g. yellow, red, blue).
4. The sidebar excerpt list can filter by color.


## 2. Underline, Strikethrough, and Wavy Line
Besides color blocks, you can use line styles.

1. On the selection toolbar, choose **Underline**, **Strikethrough**, or **Wavy line**.
2. Good when you want tone differences without a full background fill.
3. Can combine with color; exact combinations depend on the current UI.

> Note: Line-style marks may be a premium excerpt capability. If prompted to unlock, follow the plugin guidance.

## 3. Thoughts
A thought is a short comment attached to an excerpt—not a separate unrelated note.

1. Select an existing highlight, or after creating an excerpt, click **Thought** on the toolbar.
2. Write your thought in the input box and save.
3. In the body, open the thought marker again to edit.
4. The sidebar can filter **with thought / without thought**.
5. In notes, thoughts often use a separator convention (e.g. `---div---`) from excerpt body text so the reader can parse them.


## 4. Excerpt Timestamps
Toggle **Excerpt timestamp** in the menu.

1. When on, newly inserted excerpts include capture time—useful for time-based review later.
2. When off, inserted content stays cleaner if you care only about the original text.
3. Export templates may also use time fields. See `Er-04a 导出笔记与模板`.


## 5. Hide Strikethrough Text
If strikethrough means **this passage is rejected / outdated**, readability suffers.

1. Choose **Hide strikethrough text** in the menu.
2. When you need comparison, show hidden text and strikethrough in the sidebar.
3. Good for close reading revisions; does not affect whether the excerpt was saved to notes.


## 6. Edit and Delete
1. Click a highlight in the body to change color, add a thought, jump to the note, or delete the highlight.
2. Deleting a highlight removes re-display in the book; whether the note paragraph is deleted depends on whether you also edited the note.
3. Safer approach: delete that excerpt in the note and let re-highlight disappear with it. Workflow in `Er-02a 摘录笔记工作流`.
