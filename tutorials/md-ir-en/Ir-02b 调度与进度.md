Scheduling decides when a reading point reappears on the calendar. It serves reading rhythm—not Weave Deck’s FSRS reviews. Basic scheduling is part of the free main flow; deep strategy customization and interleaving are premium.

## Two strategies
Settings labels: **Processing** and **Reading list**.

| Strategy | Tendency | Fits |
|----------|----------|------|
| **Processing** | Prefer active material; fewer context switches | Short-cycle work on the same theme |
| **Reading list** | More “book list” feel; less repeated popping of the same chunk | Once-a-day, read-along pace |

Ship the add → resume → progress loop first, then tune.

## Common rhythm controls
Under Settings → Core scheduling / Advanced: daily time budget, daily reading-point cap, flow stretch, auto defer under load, cross-day smoothing, ahead window and interval-related options. Calendar day-load summaries often show baseline, stretch ceiling, and assigned minutes.

## Point actions and states
1. **Open / resume** — jump to source.
2. **Mark progress** — compute next appearance by strategy.
3. **Reschedule** — set or move the day.
4. **Priority** — relative importance in the queue.
5. **Suspend** — leave the main today queue; restore from continue-reading suggestions.
6. **Archive** — end active scheduling; Markdown sources may get archive-related tags for vault filters.
7. **Remove** — drop from topic/queue (confirm first).

## Subscription and “today” pins
**Add to today** pins on today; **Schedule normally** spreads by horizon/load. Don’t confuse the two and assume a bug.

## Optional premium surfaces
Scheduling strategy settings, interleaving, reading timer, and analytics are premium. **Tag groups** are still being improved—not a daily dependency.
