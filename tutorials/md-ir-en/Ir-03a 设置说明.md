Open Settings → Weave Incremental Reading. Typical tabs: Basic, Core scheduling, Advanced, License, About. Enable Show advanced feature preview on Basic to see locked premium entries.

## Basic
Language, advanced feature preview, data folder (vault data root), **Markdown focused reading** (free), data management entry, **Subscribe clipping folders** (premium). Back up before changing the data root.

## Core scheduling
Daily time budget, daily reading-point cap, ahead window, interval-related options, **strategy** (Processing / Reading list), flow stretch, auto defer, cross-day smoothing, defaults for new points (e.g. inbox topic). Some deep strategy knobs may be license-gated.

## Advanced
Interleaving, bury/pull-back, auto postpone, priority half-life, and more; **Tag group manager** (premium, still being improved—not a daily workflow); tag ↔ YAML sync options.

## License
View status; activate standalone; inherit from an activated Weave Deck when supported.

## Calendar preferences
Some options live under calendar More or list prefs: continuous reading, schedule preview, view modes, show timers/type/missing-source, hide completed/archived, background wall (premium). Follow your installed build’s menus.
